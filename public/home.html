What you get

One app for requests, offers and the calendar.

Every business gets its own page, inbox, logins, colors and logo.

Your questions, your form

Ask exactly what you need: photos, addresses, dates, guest counts, sizes. Change the wording any time.

Offers and counter-offers

Send a price, date and terms. Clients accept, ask a question or counter. Once it's agreed, it's booked.

Instant booking

Barbershops and salons: clients pick a service, a barber and an open time, and they're booked on the spot.

Calendar sync

Works alongside the calendar you already use, so you never double-book.

Texts and reminders

Confirmations and day-before reminders go out by email and text. Clients can move or cancel from the link.

Your team and clients

Logins for every barber, artist or tech, a client list you can import, and a dashboard for the owner.
