Sync your calendar

Connect the calendar you already use, so online booking never lands on top of something you've got going on.

How it works

Beam checks your calendar every few minutes and treats anything on it as busy. Clients booking online only see open times. They never see what's on your calendar.

If you still take bookings in Squire, connect the calendar Squire puts your appointments into, so nothing gets double-booked while both are running.

1. Get your calendar link

iPhone (iCloud calendar)
  1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the ⓘ next to the calendar your appointments are on. It has to be under iCloud, not "On My iPhone".
  3. Turn on Public Calendar, then tap Share Link… → Copy.
  4. Paste it below. It starts with webcal://.
Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com → the gear icon → Settings.
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.
  4. Paste it below.
Squire

If Squire is set to send your appointments to your iPhone or Google calendar, connect that calendar using the steps above. If Squire gives you its own calendar link (starts with webcal:// or ends in .ics), you can paste that instead.
