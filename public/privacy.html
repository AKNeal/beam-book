Privacy policy and text message terms

This shop · online booking run by Neal Media & Production (Beam) · Last updated November 18, 2026

What we collect

When you book online with the shop, we collect your name, mobile phone number, email (if you give it), the appointment you choose, and any note you add. If you pay by card, Stripe processes the payment; we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you about, change or cancel your appointment.
  • To let the shop and your barber contact you about your appointment.
  • To keep the shop's appointment history and receipts.

Who we share it with

Only the shop and the services that run the booking system for it: Neal Media & Production, and its providers for hosting, email (Resend), text messages (Twilio) and payments (Stripe). We don't sell your information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Text message terms

  • What you'll get: if you tick "Text me" when booking, the shop texts you about your appointments: a confirmation, a reminder the day before, and any change or cancellation.
  • How often: message frequency varies, usually 2 to 4 texts per appointment.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Stop any time: reply STOP to stop texts. Reply START to get them again.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact the shop at the number on its website.
  • Texting is optional and not required to book. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your choices

You can ask the shop to update or delete your information at any time.
